훅

원문 제목: Money needs a hook, not a prompt (CHOOSING THE TOOL). A prompt rule: "Never refund more than $500" works most of the time. / A hook: Makes sure it always happens. 하단: Guaranteed compliance: choose a hook, not a prompt rule.
