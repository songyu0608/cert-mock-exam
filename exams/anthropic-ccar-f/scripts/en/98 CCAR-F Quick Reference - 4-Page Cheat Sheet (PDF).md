# 98. CCAR-F Quick Reference: 4-Page Cheat Sheet (PDF)

This lecture gives you the CCAR-F Quick Reference: a 4-page PDF to review the whole course before the exam.

Download it

Open Resources for this lecture and download CCAR-F-Quick-Reference.pdf. It prints on four A4 pages. You can also keep it open on a second screen when you watch a lecture again.

Why it helps

The exam describes a real situation and asks for the best action. To answer well, you need the exact names, values, files and flags. You also need to see why the tempting wrong option fails. The sheet puts all of this on four pages, in the same order as the course.

What is inside

- The exam in one block: 60 questions, 120 minutes, the passing score and the weight of each domain, plus the six scenarios.
- The decision routine: how to read a question and choose the answer, as one diagram.
- One block for each section of the course, with the facts to know by heart.
- Five diagrams: the decision routine; a Message Batches timeline for a 30-hour deadline; the agentic loop; gates and hooks; the coordinator and its subagents.
- 39 traps: wrong options that look right, each with the right move.
- 5 “Guide vs today” notes (see below).
- A short list of topics that are not on the exam, so you don't spend time on them.

How to read it

- L12 next to a line is the number of the lecture in this course that explains it. If a line is not clear, watch that lecture again.
- A red TRAP line starts with a wrong option that looks right. The arrow → points to the right move.
- A green GUIDE VS TODAY line marks a place where the official Exam Guide (July 2026) and today's product are different. On the exam, answer as the guide says. In your own code, follow the current documentation.
- In the diagrams, orange boxes are Claude and the words it reads. Dark boxes are your code. This is the idea behind most answers: words guide Claude, but a rule that must always hold goes in code.

How to use it

1. After each section, read its block. Try to explain each line in your own words. If you can't, watch the lecture with that L number again.
2. After a question you got wrong, find its topic on the sheet and read the traps next to it. A wrong answer usually matches one of them.
3. The day before the exam, read all four pages once, slowly. Spend most of the time on the first diagram and the traps.

Don't learn the sheet as a list. The exam rarely asks for a definition: it gives you a situation and asks what to do. The sheet helps you remember the facts. The lectures help you understand them.

Good to know

- The sheet was made for this course. It is not an official Anthropic document.
- It follows the official Exam Guide (July 2026) and the Claude products as of October 2026.
- Fees, cancellation and retake rules change. Check the current exam policy before you book.
