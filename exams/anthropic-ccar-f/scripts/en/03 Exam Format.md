# 3. Exam Format

Now, let's take a closer look at the structure and format of the exam.

The Claude Certified Architect Foundations exam contains 60 questions, and you have 120 minutes to complete it. That gives you an average of about two minutes per question.

The exam includes both multiple-choice and multiple-response questions. For multiple-choice questions, you select the single best answer. For multiple-response questions, you may need to select more than one answer. The question will always tell you how many responses to select.

The exam is built around realistic scenarios. During the exam, you will receive four scenarios selected at random from a bank of six. The six possible scenarios cover a customer support resolution agent, code generation with Claude Code, a multi-agent research system, developer productivity with Claude, Claude Code in a continuous integration pipeline, and structured data extraction.

Multiple questions can be based on the same scenario. This means the exam is not primarily about memorizing isolated facts. You need to understand the situation, identify the important requirements and constraints, and choose the most appropriate solution.

The exam covers five domains. Agentic Architecture and Orchestration represents 27 percent of the exam. Tool Design and Model Context Protocol Integration represents 18 percent. Claude Code Configuration and Workflows represents 20 percent. Prompt Engineering and Structured Output represents another 20 percent. And Context Management and Reliability represents the remaining 15 percent.

The exam uses scaled scoring. Scores range from 100 to 1,000, and the minimum passing score is 720. A score of 720 does not mean that you need exactly 72 percent of the questions correct. The exam uses a scaled scoring model so that results can remain comparable across different versions of the exam that may vary slightly in difficulty.

After the exam, you receive a pass or fail result along with your scaled score. Your score report also shows the percentage of questions you answered correctly in each domain. These domain percentages are provided to help you understand your performance. They do not independently determine whether you pass or fail.

The standard exam fee is 125 US dollars for each attempt. Registration begins through the Anthropic Partner Academy, and the exam itself is delivered through Pearson VUE.

If you work for an organization that participates in the Claude Partner Network, I recommend using your corporate email address when accessing the Partner Academy and registering for the certification. This helps associate your certification activity with the correct partner organization. Using a personal email address can make that association less straightforward and may require additional account or registration corrections later.

Your organization's partner status can also affect the price you pay. Tiered partners may receive a discounted rate on their first exam attempt, and any discount available to your organization is reflected during checkout. So before paying the standard price, make sure you are using the correct account associated with your employer and check whether your organization participates in the Claude Partner Network.

After registration, you follow the provided instructions to create or access your Pearson VUE account and schedule the exam. You can take the exam through online proctoring or at an eligible Pearson VUE test center.

If you need to cancel or reschedule your exam, make sure you do it at least 48 hours before your appointment. Exams cannot be canceled or rescheduled less than 48 hours before the scheduled time, and missing this deadline may result in losing your exam fee.

If you do not pass the exam, you can take it again, but there are mandatory waiting periods. After the first failed attempt, you must wait 14 days. After the second failed attempt, you must wait 30 days. And after the third failed attempt, you must wait 90 days. You can take the same exam a maximum of four times within a rolling 12-month period, and the exam fee applies to every attempt.

Once you pass, the certification is valid for 12 months. To renew it on time, you can complete a free, non-proctored renewal assessment. If the certification expires before you renew it, you must take the full exam again and pay the full exam fee.

There is one important thing to remember when answering questions. Several answer choices may sound technically possible. Your goal is not simply to find an answer that could work. Look carefully at the requirements, constraints, and problems described in the scenario. Eliminate answers that do not address the actual root cause, introduce unnecessary complexity, or violate the stated requirements. Then choose the solution that best fits the situation. That kind of practical architectural judgment is a major part of this exam.
